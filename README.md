# Straznik-kosmosu--team-project--showcase
Showcase repository for "Strażnik kosmosu" team developed game

Game title - Strażnik Kosmosu (eng. The Space Guardian)

Game description - A 3D Unity FPS university team project where you play as a soldier defending space stations across three hazardous planets from an alien threat. Armed with a blue-glowing kinetic gun, your objective is to survive for 3 minutes per planet while clearing out distinct enemy types — heavy tanks on Planet 1, agile ranged shooters on Planet 2, and flying UFOs combined with environmental lava hazards on Planet 3. Manage your limited health and survive all three levels to win. It's an university team work project from 2023.

My responsibilities in this project:
- design and implementation of Level 1 and 2 environments,
- design and implementation of Level 1 enemies,
- asset selection,
- initial game concept and pre-design,
- co-testing
